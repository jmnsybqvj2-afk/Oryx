# Video prompts by scene

> For **Kling**, use `07-kling-shots.md` instead: those prompts are ready to paste. This file is
> for other video tools.

Replace [STYLE], [ORYX], [LUNA], [SAUCER] and [RED ANTENNAE] with the blocks in
`../shared/characters-and-style.md`.

**Scene 1 · Opening:** use the clip in `../shared/opening-and-closing-songs.md`.

**Scene 2 · Playing ball (20 s)**
```
[STYLE] Bright sunny garden with a big shady tree, a big cartoon sun in the sky. [ORYX]
throws a red ball and [LUNA] runs to fetch it happily. Then Luna walks back slowly, sits
down and pants with her little tongue out, looking tired and hot.
```

**Scene 3 · Beep-beep! (15 s)**
```
[STYLE] Close-up of [ORYX] in the sunny garden, [RED ANTENNAE]. He looks down at [LUNA]
panting with her tongue out, and tilts his head, curious.
```

**Scene 4 · Oryx gets it wrong (20 s)**
```
[STYLE] [ORYX] offers a shiny star-shaped glowing space cookie to [LUNA]. Luna turns her
face away and shakes her head. Oryx looks at the cookie, confused.
```

**Scene 5 · Question for the kids (10 s)**
```
[STYLE] [ORYX] looks directly at the camera with a curious face. Three big floating icons
appear next to him: a cookie, a water drop and a teddy bear. After a pause, the water drop
glows and grows bigger.
```

**Scene 6 · Water! (20 s)**
```
[STYLE] [ORYX] pours fresh water from a jug into a small blue bowl. [LUNA] drinks happily,
little water drops splashing, her tail wagging fast.
```

**Scene 7 · Into the shade (20 s)**
```
[STYLE] [ORYX] and [LUNA] lie down in the cool shade under a big green tree, Luna wags her
tail relaxed, Oryx fans himself with his hand and smiles, leaves moving gently in the breeze.
```

**Scene 8 · Song (75 s, 4 clips)**
```
Clip A (verse 1): [STYLE] A big cartoon sun shines strongly, [LUNA] pants with her
tongue out, heat waves drawn softly in the air.
Clip B (chorus): [STYLE] [LUNA] drinks from her bowl splashing sparkly drops, [ORYX] jumps
and dances happily next to her.
Clip C (verse 2): [STYLE] [ORYX] offers a star-shaped space cookie, it bounces back
funny, Oryx laughs and gives Luna water instead.
Clip D (verse 3 and final chorus): [STYLE] [ORYX] and [LUNA] dance together in the shade of
the big tree, music notes and water drops floating around.
```

**Scene 9 · Celebration and sticker (15 s)**
```
[STYLE] [ORYX] jumps and roars happily, wiggling his dinosaur tail. He opens a small
colorful notebook and sticks a shiny puppy sticker with a water drop inside, sparkles
appear. [LUNA] barks happily.
```

**Scene 10 · What did we learn? (10 s)**
```
[STYLE] [ORYX] and [LUNA] sit in the shade under the tree with the water bowl next to
them, looking at the camera and talking happily.
```

**Scene 11 · Closing:** use the clip in `../shared/opening-and-closing-songs.md`.

## Voice prompts (if you use AI voices)
- **Oryx:** `Young child voice, curious, cheerful, slightly high-pitched, amazed tone, Spanish.`
- **Luna:** `Sweet small female voice, gentle and brave, Spanish.`
