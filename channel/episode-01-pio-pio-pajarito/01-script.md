# Episode 1 — "The Little Bird Who Fell from the Nest"

**Total length:** ≈ 4:00 · **Lesson:** little animals like to be with their family; if you find
an animal that needs help, tell a grown-up first.
**Characters:** Oryx, Luna, Rosa the veterinarian, the baby bird and its mother.
**Place:** a park with a big tree.

> This file is **for reading the story only** (not for Kling, not for ElevenLabs).
> The dialogue here is the English version. The **Spanish dialogue that is recorded** is in `08-voices.md`.

---

### Scene 1 · 0:00–0:15 · Opening song
See `../shared/opening-and-closing-songs.md`.

### Scene 1b · Narrator welcome (≈ 10 s)
[ON SCREEN: the flying saucer and Luna clip (`../shared/presentation/flying-saucer-and-luna-16x9.mp4`) or the official image with a slow zoom. No new Kling shot needed.]
**Narrator:** Hello, little friends! Welcome to the adventures of Oryx and Luna. Today their mission is to rescue a baby bird, and we'll learn new words like bird, nest and feathers. Parents: remember to keep an eye on the content your children watch; it's important. And now, let's play!

### Scene 2 · 0:15–0:30 · Walk in the park
[ON SCREEN: sunny morning in the park. Oryx walks touching the grass in amazement. Luna trots beside him.]
**Oryx:** Luna, look! This green stuff is so soft… what is it called?
**Luna:** Grass, Oryx! It's called grass.
**Oryx:** Grass! There's no grass on my planet! RAWR!
[ON SCREEN: Oryx wiggles his tail.]

### Scene 3 · 0:30–0:45 · Beep-beep!
[ON SCREEN: Oryx's antennae light up red and blink.]
**Oryx:** Beep-beep-beep! My little antennae! Someone needs help!
[ON SCREEN: Oryx and Luna look around. On the grass, a small gray baby bird is chirping.]
**Oryx:** There! A little ball with feathers!

### Scene 4 · 0:45–1:05 · Oryx gets it wrong
[ON SCREEN: Oryx walks over excitedly and reaches out his arms.]
**Oryx:** I know! I'll take him to my ship and play with him all day!
**Luna:** Woof, woof!
[ON SCREEN: Luna stands in front of the baby bird and shakes her head.]
**Oryx:** No? But… he's so tiny and cute!

### Scene 5 · 1:05–1:15 · Question for the kids
[ON SCREEN: Oryx looks straight at the camera.]
**Oryx:** Do you know what the little bird needs?
[ON SCREEN: 3-second pause with a big question mark and soft waiting music.]

### Scene 6 · 1:15–1:35 · Luna translates
[ON SCREEN: Luna listens with her ears up. The baby bird chirps, looking up.]
**Luna:** He says he misses his mommy. His little home is up there!
[ON SCREEN: the camera slowly moves up the tree to a nest. The mother bird is calling.]
**Oryx:** Oh! He has a mommy who's looking for him!

### Scene 7 · 1:35–2:00 · Asking a grown-up for help
**Oryx:** We need to ask a grown-up for help!
**Luna:** Woof! Here comes Rosa, the veterinarian!
[ON SCREEN: Rosa arrives. She puts on her gloves, picks up the baby bird very carefully and puts it back in the nest.]
**Oryx:** What's a veterinarian?
**Rosa:** She's the animals' doctor! Well done, Oryx. When you find a little animal, you tell a grown-up first.
**Oryx:** Thank you, Rosa!

### Scene 8 · 2:00–3:15 · Episode song
See `02-song.md`. Images for each part:
- **Verse 1:** animated replay of Oryx finding the baby bird and his antennae lighting up.
- **Chorus:** the baby bird snuggled with its mother in the nest; Oryx and Luna flap their "wings".
- **Verse 2:** Luna barks and Rosa puts the baby bird back in the nest.
- **Final chorus:** everyone flaps their arms like wings; little hearts around the nest.

### Scene 9 · 3:15–3:30 · Celebration and sticker
[ON SCREEN: mother bird and baby together. Oryx jumps for joy.]
[ON SCREEN: Oryx roars happily.]
**Oryx:** RAWR! A little bird sticker for my Earthling Diary!
[ON SCREEN: Oryx opens the diary and sticks in a sparkly baby bird sticker.]

### Scene 10 · 3:30–3:40 · What did we learn today?
**Oryx:** What did we learn today?
**Luna:** That little animals like to be with their family!
**Oryx:** And that we tell a grown-up first!

### Scene 10b · Narrator reflection (≈ 8 s)
[ON SCREEN: Oryx and Luna with the Earthling Diary, or the official image with a slow zoom. No new Kling shot needed.]
**Narrator:** Remember, little friend: if a little animal needs help, tell a grown-up. And don't forget to brush your teeth and wash your little hands! See you on the next adventure, little friends!
