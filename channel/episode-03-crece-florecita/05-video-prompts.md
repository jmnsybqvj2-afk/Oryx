# Video prompts by scene

> For **Kling**, use `07-kling-shots.md` instead: those prompts are ready to paste. This file is
> for other video tools.

Replace [STYLE], [ORYX], [LUNA], [SAUCER] and [RED ANTENNAE] with the blocks in
`../shared/characters-and-style.md`.

**Scene 1 · Opening:** use the clip in `../shared/opening-and-closing-songs.md`.

**Scene 2 · The garden of colors (20 s)**
```
[STYLE] A cheerful garden full of red, blue, pink and purple flowers, a round shiny yellow
flying saucer [SAUCER] parked at one side. [ORYX] walks among the flowers pointing at them amazed,
[LUNA] sniffs the flowers. Camera slowly reveals one sad droopy gray flower in the shadow
of the flying saucer.
```

**Scene 3 · Beep-beep! (15 s)**
```
[STYLE] Close-up of [ORYX], [RED ANTENNAE]. He looks at a sad droopy gray flower,
confused. [LUNA] sits next to the flower looking up at Oryx.
```

**Scene 4 · Oryx gets it wrong (20 s)**
```
[STYLE] [ORYX] plays a funny air guitar in front of a sad gray flower, his antennae
bouncing, cartoon music notes flying around. The flower stays droopy. Oryx stops and
shrugs, a little disappointed. [LUNA] tilts her head.
```

**Scene 5 · Question for the kids (10 s)**
```
[STYLE] [ORYX] looks directly at the camera with a curious face. Three big floating icons
appear: a guitar, a water drop and a smiling sun. After a pause, the water drop and the sun
glow brighter.
```

**Scene 6 · Luna finds the problem (20 s)**
```
[STYLE] [LUNA] sniffs dry cracked soil around a droopy gray flower. The camera pans up to
show the big flying saucer blocking the sunlight and casting a shadow on the flower.
[ORYX] covers his mouth with both hands, surprised.
```

**Scene 7 · Water and sun (20 s)**
```
[STYLE] The flying saucer floats a little to the side and warm sunlight shines on
a gray flower. [ORYX] and [LUNA] water it with a small watering can, gentle drops falling.
The flower slowly starts to lift up, still gray.
```

**Scene 8 · Song (80 s, 5 clips)**
```
Clip A (verse 1): [STYLE] Soft-focus memory: a sad gray flower and [ORYX] playing air
guitar, music notes floating.
Clip B (verse 2): [STYLE] [LUNA] sniffs dry soil, sparkly water drops fall one by one on
the flower.
Clip C (chorus): [STYLE] A flower slowly grows taller in the sunlight; [ORYX] and [LUNA] rise
up from a crouch with it, opening their arms.
Clip D (colors): [STYLE] Magical transformation: the flower changes color step by step,
red, then yellow, then pink, then purple, then blue, sparkles burst at each change, ending
as a big bright rainbow-colored flower. [ORYX] claps amazed.
Clip E (final chorus): [STYLE] The whole garden shines with colors, [ORYX] and [LUNA] dance
happily around the beautiful flower.
```

**Scene 9 · Celebration and sticker (15 s)**
```
[STYLE] [ORYX] jumps and roars happily, wiggling his dinosaur tail. He opens a small
colorful notebook and sticks a shiny rainbow flower sticker inside, sparkles appear.
[LUNA] barks happily.
```

**Scene 10 · What did we learn? (10 s)**
```
[STYLE] [ORYX] and [LUNA] sit next to the bright colorful flower in the sunny garden,
looking at the camera and talking happily, Oryx laughs.
```

**Scene 11 · Closing:** use the clip in `../shared/opening-and-closing-songs.md`.

## Voice prompts (if you use AI voices)
- **Oryx:** `Young child voice, curious, cheerful, slightly high-pitched, amazed tone, Spanish.`
- **Luna:** `Sweet small female voice, gentle and brave, Spanish.`
