# Songs in Suno — ready to paste

In Suno, use **Custom** mode. Each song has three boxes: **Lyrics**, **Style** and **Title**.
"Instrumental" must be **off** (the songs have vocals).

In the lyrics the name is written **"Oris"** so Suno sings it correctly.
Suno makes longer songs than we need: cut them to length in CapCut.
Each generation gives 2 versions: pick the best one.
