# Episode 2 — "Luna Is Thirsty"

**Total length:** ≈ 4:00 · **Lesson:** our pets need fresh water and shade when it's hot.
**Characters:** Oryx and Luna.
**Place:** a garden with a big tree, on a very sunny day.

> This file is **for reading the story only** (not for Kling, not for ElevenLabs).
> The dialogue here is the English version. The **Spanish dialogue that is recorded** is in `08-voices.md`.

---

### Scene 1 · 0:00–0:15 · Opening song
See `../shared/opening-and-closing-songs.md`.

### Scene 1b · Narrator welcome (≈ 15 s)
[ON SCREEN: the flying saucer and Luna clip (`../shared/presentation/flying-saucer-and-luna-16x9.mp4`) or the official image with a slow zoom. No new Kling shot needed.]
**Narrator:** Hello, little friends! Welcome! Today it's very hot and Luna needs help. We'll learn what our pets need when it's hot, and also the words hot and cold, sun and shade. Parents: today your children will learn that pets always need fresh water and a shady place. Ready? Let's go!

### Scene 2 · 0:15–0:35 · Playing ball
[ON SCREEN: garden with a big sun. Oryx throws a red ball; Luna runs and brings it back.]
**Oryx:** Again, Luna! Again!
[ON SCREEN: Luna brings the ball back more slowly, sits down and sticks out her little tongue, panting.]

### Scene 3 · 0:35–0:50 · Beep-beep!
[ON SCREEN: Oryx's antennae light up red.]
**Oryx:** Beep-beep-beep! Luna? My antennae are beeping for you!
**Oryx:** Your tongue is sticking out! Is it a new game?

### Scene 4 · 0:50–1:10 · Oryx gets it wrong
**Oryx:** I know! I'll give you my space cookie!
[ON SCREEN: Oryx offers her a shiny star-shaped cookie. Luna turns her face away.]
**Luna:** Woof!
[ON SCREEN: Luna shakes her head.]
**Oryx:** You don't want a cookie? Hmm…

### Scene 5 · 1:10–1:20 · Question for the kids
[ON SCREEN: Oryx looks at the camera. Three big options appear: 🍪 cookie, 💧 water, 🧸 toy.]
**Oryx:** Do you know what Luna needs when it's very hot?
[ON SCREEN: 3-second pause. Then the 💧 glows and grows.]

### Scene 6 · 1:20–1:40 · Water!
**Oryx:** Water?! Nice fresh water!
**Luna:** Woof! Yes, Oryx! When it's hot, us doggies need water.
[ON SCREEN: Oryx fills the bowl with a jug. Luna drinks happily: "glug, glug, glug".]

### Scene 7 · 1:40–2:00 · Into the shade
**Luna:** And some shade too, to rest from the sun!
[ON SCREEN: they both lie down under the tree. Luna wags her tail; Oryx fans himself with his hand.]
**Oryx:** Aaah, it's so nice and cool!

### Scene 8 · 2:00–3:15 · Episode song
See `02-song.md`. Images for each part:
- **Verse 1:** the sun is burning hot, Luna with her tongue out.
- **Chorus:** Luna drinks and splashes little drops; Oryx jumps and dances.
- **Verse 2:** the space cookie bounces back, Oryx laughs.
- **Verse 3 and final chorus:** both under the tree, dancing in the shade.

### Scene 9 · 3:15–3:30 · Celebration and sticker
**Oryx:** RAWR! A puppy sticker for my Earthling Diary!
[ON SCREEN: he sticks in a puppy sticker with a water drop.]

### Scene 10 · 3:30–3:40 · What did we learn today?
**Oryx:** What did we learn today?
**Luna:** That when it's hot, us pets need fresh water and shade!

### Scene 10b · Narrator reflection (≈ 15 s)
[ON SCREEN: Oryx and Luna with the Earthling Diary, or the official image with a slow zoom. No new Kling shot needed.]
**Narrator:** Remember, little friend: when it's hot, you need to drink water too, just like Luna. And if you have a pet, check with a grown-up that its bowl always has fresh water. See you on the next adventure!

See `../shared/opening-and-closing-songs.md`.
