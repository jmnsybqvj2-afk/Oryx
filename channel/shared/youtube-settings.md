# Settings for uploading each video

## Required
- **Audience: "Yes, it's made for kids".** This turns off comments, bell notifications and
  personalized ads. It is required by law (COPPA) for this kind of content.
- **Altered or synthetic content:** if you use AI for clearly unrealistic cartoon animation, you
  usually don't need to mark it. If something looks real (real people or places), you do.
  Check the option in YouTube Studio on every upload.

## For every video
- Title and thumbnail from `03-title-and-thumbnail.md`.
- Description and tags from `04-description-and-tags.md`.
- Video language: Spanish. Upload Spanish subtitles (they help with search).
- Add the episode to the **"Oryx y Luna — Episodios"** playlist and the standalone song to
  **"Oryx y Luna — Canciones"**.
- Always publish on the same day and at the same time.

## Quality (so you don't lose monetization)
YouTube rewards educational kids' content made with care, and may limit monetization of
repetitive or mass-produced content. Every episode must have its own story, lesson and song.
Avoid uploading near-identical variations of the same video.

## What not to do
- Don't ask kids to subscribe, like or buy anything.
- Don't put links to outside websites aimed at kids.
- Don't show kids touching wild animals without a grown-up.
- No character carries weapons. Check every shot before using it.
