# Prompts de video por escena

Reemplaza [ESTILO], [ORYX], [LUNA] y [ANTENAS ROJAS] por los bloques de
`../comun/personajes-y-estilo.md`.

**Escena 1 · Entrada:** usar el clip de `../comun/cancion-entrada-salida.md`.

**Escena 2 · El jardín de colores (20 s)**
```
[ESTILO] A cheerful garden full of red, blue, pink and purple flowers, a round shiny yellow
spaceship parked at one side. [ORYX] walks among the flowers pointing at them amazed,
[LUNA] sniffs the flowers. Camera slowly reveals one sad droopy gray flower in the shadow
of the spaceship.
```

**Escena 3 · ¡Bip-bip! (15 s)**
```
[ESTILO] Close-up of [ORYX], [ANTENAS ROJAS]. He looks at a sad droopy gray flower,
confused. [LUNA] sits next to the flower looking up at Oryx.
```

**Escena 4 · Oryx se equivoca (20 s)**
```
[ESTILO] [ORYX] plays a funny air guitar in front of a sad gray flower, his antennae
bouncing, cartoon music notes flying around. The flower stays droopy. Oryx stops and
shrugs, a little disappointed. [LUNA] tilts her head.
```

**Escena 5 · Pregunta a los niños (10 s)**
```
[ESTILO] [ORYX] looks directly at the camera with a curious face. Three big floating icons
appear: a guitar, a water drop and a smiling sun. After a pause, the water drop and the sun
glow brighter.
```

**Escena 6 · Luna descubre el problema (20 s)**
```
[ESTILO] [LUNA] sniffs dry cracked soil around a droopy gray flower. The camera pans up to
show the big yellow spaceship blocking the sunlight and casting a shadow on the flower.
[ORYX] covers his mouth with both hands, surprised.
```

**Escena 7 · Agua y sol (20 s)**
```
[ESTILO] The round yellow spaceship floats a little to the side and warm sunlight shines on
a gray flower. [ORYX] and [LUNA] water it with a small watering can, gentle drops falling.
The flower slowly starts to lift up, still gray.
```

**Escena 8 · Canción (80 s, 5 clips)**
```
Clip A (estrofa 1): [ESTILO] Soft-focus memory: a sad gray flower and [ORYX] playing air
guitar, music notes floating.
Clip B (estrofa 2): [ESTILO] [LUNA] sniffs dry soil, sparkly water drops fall one by one on
the flower.
Clip C (coro): [ESTILO] A flower slowly grows taller in the sunlight; [ORYX] and [LUNA] rise
up from a crouch with it, opening their arms.
Clip D (colores): [ESTILO] Magical transformation: the flower changes color step by step,
red, then yellow, then pink, then purple, then blue, sparkles burst at each change, ending
as a big bright rainbow-colored flower. [ORYX] claps amazed.
Clip E (coro final): [ESTILO] The whole garden shines with colors, [ORYX] and [LUNA] dance
happily around the beautiful flower.
```

**Escena 9 · Celebración y estampita (15 s)**
```
[ESTILO] [ORYX] jumps and roars happily, wiggling his dinosaur tail. He opens a small
colorful notebook and sticks a shiny rainbow flower sticker inside, sparkles appear.
[LUNA] barks happily.
```

**Escena 10 · ¿Qué aprendimos? (10 s)**
```
[ESTILO] [ORYX] and [LUNA] sit next to the bright colorful flower in the sunny garden,
looking at the camera and talking happily, Oryx laughs.
```

**Escena 11 · Salida:** usar el clip de `../comun/cancion-entrada-salida.md`.

## Prompts de voz
- **Oryx:** `Young child voice, curious, cheerful, slightly high-pitched, amazed tone, Spanish.`
- **Luna:** `Sweet small female voice, gentle and brave, Spanish.`
