# Video prompts by scene

> For **Kling**, use `07-kling-shots.md` instead: those prompts are ready to paste. This file is
> for other video tools.

Replace [ESTILO], [ORYX], [LUNA], [ROSA] and [ANTENAS ROJAS] with the blocks in
`../comun/personajes-y-estilo.md`. Most tools make clips of 5 to 10 seconds: a long scene may
need 2 or 3 clips with the same prompt and small changes.

**Scene 1 · Opening:** use the clip in `../comun/cancion-entrada-salida.md`.

**Scene 2 · Walk (15 s)**
```
[ESTILO] Sunny morning in a colorful park with a big tree. [ORYX] walks slowly, touching
the soft green grass with wonder and smiling. [LUNA] trots happily beside him. Then Oryx
jumps with joy and wiggles his dinosaur tail.
```

**Scene 3 · Beep-beep! (15 s)**
```
[ESTILO] Close-up of [ORYX] in the park, [ANTENAS ROJAS]. He looks around surprised.
Cut to: a tiny fluffy gray baby bird sitting alone on the grass, chirping, looking small and sad.
[LUNA] perks up her ears.
```

**Scene 4 · Oryx gets it wrong (20 s)**
```
[ESTILO] [ORYX] leans toward a tiny gray baby bird on the grass with excited open arms.
[LUNA] gently steps in front of the bird and shakes her head "no". Oryx stops, confused,
and scratches his hood.
```

**Scene 5 · Question for the kids (10 s)**
```
[ESTILO] Medium shot of [ORYX] looking directly at the camera with a curious face, one
finger on his chin, a big friendly colorful question mark floating next to him.
```

**Scene 6 · Luna translates (20 s)**
```
[ESTILO] [LUNA] listens to a tiny gray baby bird with her big ears up. Slow camera tilt up the
tree trunk to a nest on a branch where a gray mother bird looks down and calls. [ORYX] looks up,
surprised and happy.
```

**Scene 7 · Rosa helps (25 s)**
```
[ESTILO] [ROSA] walks over smiling, puts on gloves and very gently lifts the tiny gray baby bird
back into the nest on a low branch. [ORYX] and [LUNA] watch with hopeful faces, then Oryx
says thank you and Rosa gives a thumbs up.
```

**Scene 8 · Song (75 s, 4 clips)**
```
Clip A (verse 1): [ESTILO] Dreamy soft-focus replay: [ORYX] finds a gray baby bird in the
grass, [ANTENAS ROJAS], music notes floating.
Clip B (chorus): [ESTILO] The gray baby bird cuddles with its mother in the nest, little hearts
float around; below, [ORYX] and [LUNA] flap their arms and paws like wings, smiling.
Clip C (verse 2): [ESTILO] [LUNA] barks happily, [ROSA] gently places the gray baby bird in
the nest.
Clip D (final chorus): [ESTILO] [ORYX], [LUNA] and [ROSA] dance flapping their arms like wings
under the tree, the birds chirp happily above, soft sparkles.
```

**Scene 9 · Celebration and sticker (15 s)**
```
[ESTILO] [ORYX] jumps with joy and roars happily, wiggling his dinosaur tail. He opens a
small colorful notebook and sticks a shiny gray baby bird sticker inside, sparkles appear.
[LUNA] wags her tail.
```

**Scene 10 · What did we learn? (10 s)**
```
[ESTILO] [ORYX] and [LUNA] sit on the grass under the tree, looking at the camera and
talking happily, the bird family in the nest in the background.
```

**Scene 11 · Closing:** use the clip in `../comun/cancion-entrada-salida.md`.

## Voice prompts (if you use AI voices)
- **Oryx:** `Young child voice, curious, cheerful, slightly high-pitched, amazed tone, Spanish.`
- **Luna:** `Sweet small female voice, gentle and brave, Spanish.`
- **Rosa:** `Warm kind adult woman voice, calm and friendly, Spanish.`
