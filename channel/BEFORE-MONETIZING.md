# ⚠️ Before monetizing the channel — checklist

Claude keeps this list so it can be reminded when the channel is close to monetizing.
When you get close (for example ~800 subscribers), tell Claude: **"vamos a monetizar"**.

## When YouTube starts paying
YouTube only pays (ads) after the channel joins the **YouTube Partner Program**. The usual
requirements are about **1,000 subscribers** plus **4,000 public watch hours in the last 12 months**
(or 10 million Shorts views in 90 days). Check the current rules in YouTube Studio → **Earn**,
because YouTube changes them. Until then, the videos earn nothing, so the free plans are fine
for starting the channel.

## What must be redone with a paid plan before monetizing
The free plans of these apps **do not allow commercial use** (videos that earn money), and a
paid plan is **not retroactive**: songs/voices made on the free plan stay "free plan" content.
Check each app's current terms before paying.

| App | What we made on the free plan | What to do before monetizing |
|---|---|---|
| **Suno** ✅ | 3 songs (opening, "Pío, pío", closing) | ✅ **Covered:** the creator has a **paid Suno membership**, so songs made while subscribed can be used commercially. Nothing to redo. Keep making new songs while the membership is active (songs made on the free plan would not be covered). |
| **ElevenLabs** | Oryx, Luna and Rosa voices (21 lines) | Paid plan (Starter or higher) for commercial use, then **regenerate the lines** (`08-voices.md`). The saved voices make it fast. |
| **Kling** | 6 clips + presentation clips | Check Kling's terms for commercial use on your plan. A paid plan also removes the "KlingAI" watermark. |
| **CapCut** | Editing | Don't use CapCut's library music in the videos; use our own songs. |

## Every time you upload
- Mark the video as **Made for Kids**.
- No weapons, nothing scary, no links for kids, no "subscribe" requests to kids.
