# Song — "Pío, pío, pajarito"

**Style:** soft and tender, like a lullaby. **Length:** 1:00–1:15. **Tempo:** slow (≈ 80 BPM).

> The lyrics are in **Spanish** (this is what is sung).

**Verse 1**
Oryx encontró en el suelo
un pajarito sin vuelo.
Sus antenitas brillaron,
¡bip, bip, bip!, le avisaron.

**Chorus**
Pío, pío, pajarito,
no estés triste, chiquitito,
a tu nido volverás,
con tu mamá y papá.

**Verse 2**
Luna ladra: "¡Guau, cuidado!
Busca un grande a tu lado."
Con cariño lo subieron
y a su nido lo volvieron.

**Chorus** (×2)

**Ending (whispered):** Pío, pío… ¡ya está con mamá y papá!

## Moves for the kids
- Arms like wings during the chorus.
- Fingers together like a little beak on "pío, pío".
- Hug yourself on "con tu mamá y papá".

## Prompt to generate the music
```
Gentle children's lullaby song in Spanish, 80 BPM, major key, soft ukulele, music box and
glockenspiel, light bird chirp sound effects, warm sweet child-like vocals, simple
repetitive melody easy for toddlers to sing, calm and tender mood, soft "bip bip bip"
synth sound in the first verse, whispered ending.
```
Paste the full Spanish lyrics into the lyrics field of the music app.

## Ways to use the song
1. Inside the episode (Scene 8).
2. A separate music video, 1:00–1:15 long, with the lyrics on screen.
3. A vertical Short, 20–30 s, with the chorus and the "wings" move.
