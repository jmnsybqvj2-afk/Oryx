# Opening song and closing song

They are the same in every episode. Generate the music once and reuse it.
The lyrics stay in **Spanish** (this is what is sung). English translation in *italics*.

## 🛸 Opening — "¡Oryx y Luna!" (15 seconds)

¡Bip, bip, bip! ¡Bip, bip, bip!
Desde el Planeta Amarillo
llegó Oryx, el marcianito,
con Luna, su perrita,
¡a la Tierra a explorar!
¡Oryx y Luna! ¡RAWR! ¡Guau!

*(Beep, beep, beep! / From the Yellow Planet / came Oryx, the little alien, / with Luna, his puppy, / to explore the Earth! / Oryx and Luna! RAWR! Woof!)*

**On screen:** the flying saucer crosses the night sky and lowers Oryx and Luna with its light beam →
the antennae glow on "bip, bip" → Oryx waves → Luna jumps beside him → logo on "¡Oryx y Luna!".
**Moves:** fingers on top of the head like antennae; dinosaur claws on "¡RAWR!".

**Music prompt:**
```
Upbeat children's TV theme song in Spanish, 15 seconds, 120 BPM, major key, bright synth
bleeps for "bip bip bip", ukulele, glockenspiel, claps, cheerful child-like vocals, catchy
and simple, ends with a playful dinosaur roar and a small dog bark.
```

**Video prompt:**
```
[STYLE] [SAUCER] flies across a starry night sky with a crescent moon and hovers over a
green hill; [ORYX] and [LUNA] float down gently in its yellow light beam and land on the
grass, [RED ANTENNAE], Oryx waves and Luna wags her tail, both smile at the camera.
```

## 👋 Closing — "¡Adiós, terrícolas!" (20 seconds)

Hoy aprendimos algo nuevo,
lo guardamos en el corazón:
a los animales y a las plantas
los cuidamos con amor.
¡Adiós, adiós, terrícolas,
pronto volveremos a jugar!
¡Bip, bip, bip! ¡Guau! ¡RAWR!

*(Today we learned something new, / we keep it in our heart: / animals and plants / we take care of with love. / Bye, bye, Earthlings, / we'll play again soon! / Beep, beep, beep! Woof! RAWR!)*

**On screen:** Oryx opens the Earthling Diary with the stickers → hands on the heart → they wave
goodbye next to the saucer, which rises a little and stays floating. It ends with Oryx falling
asleep (`presentation/oryx-good-night-16x9.mp4`).
**Moves:** hands on the heart; wave goodbye.

**Music prompt:**
```
Gentle warm children's goodbye song in Spanish, 20 seconds, 95 BPM, major key, soft
ukulele, glockenspiel and light strings, sweet child-like vocals, calm and loving,
ends with soft "bip bip bip", a small bark and a gentle playful roar.
```

**Video prompt:**
```
[STYLE] Night in a green park under a crescent moon and stars, [ORYX] and [LUNA] stand next
to the flying saucer, Oryx holds a small notebook full of colorful animal stickers, both put a
hand and paw on their heart and wave goodbye to the camera, the flying saucer floats a little
above the ground glowing softly.
```
