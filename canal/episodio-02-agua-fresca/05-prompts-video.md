# Prompts de video por escena

Reemplaza [ESTILO], [ORYX], [LUNA] y [ANTENAS ROJAS] por los bloques de
`../comun/personajes-y-estilo.md`.

**Escena 1 · Entrada:** usar el clip de `../comun/cancion-entrada-salida.md`.

**Escena 2 · Jugando a la pelota (20 s)**
```
[ESTILO] Bright sunny garden with a big shady tree, a big cartoon sun in the sky. [ORYX]
throws a red ball and [LUNA] runs to fetch it happily. Then Luna walks back slowly, sits
down and pants with her little tongue out, looking tired and hot.
```

**Escena 3 · ¡Bip-bip! (15 s)**
```
[ESTILO] Close-up of [ORYX] in the sunny garden, [ANTENAS ROJAS]. He looks down at [LUNA]
panting with her tongue out, and tilts his head, curious.
```

**Escena 4 · Oryx se equivoca (20 s)**
```
[ESTILO] [ORYX] offers a shiny star-shaped glowing space cookie to [LUNA]. Luna turns her
face away and shakes her head. Oryx looks at the cookie, confused.
```

**Escena 5 · Pregunta a los niños (10 s)**
```
[ESTILO] [ORYX] looks directly at the camera with a curious face. Three big floating icons
appear next to him: a cookie, a water drop and a teddy bear. After a pause, the water drop
glows and grows bigger.
```

**Escena 6 · ¡Agua! (20 s)**
```
[ESTILO] [ORYX] pours fresh water from a jug into a small blue bowl. [LUNA] drinks happily,
little water drops splashing, her tail wagging fast.
```

**Escena 7 · A la sombrita (20 s)**
```
[ESTILO] [ORYX] and [LUNA] lie down in the cool shade under a big green tree, Luna wags her
tail relaxed, Oryx fans himself with his hand and smiles, leaves moving gently in the breeze.
```

**Escena 8 · Canción (75 s, 4 clips)**
```
Clip A (estrofa 1): [ESTILO] A big cartoon sun shines strongly, [LUNA] pants with her
tongue out, heat waves drawn softly in the air.
Clip B (coro): [ESTILO] [LUNA] drinks from her bowl splashing sparkly drops, [ORYX] jumps
and dances happily next to her.
Clip C (estrofa 2): [ESTILO] [ORYX] offers a star-shaped space cookie, it bounces back
funny, Oryx laughs and gives Luna water instead.
Clip D (estrofa 3 y coro final): [ESTILO] [ORYX] and [LUNA] dance together in the shade of
the big tree, music notes and water drops floating around.
```

**Escena 9 · Celebración y estampita (15 s)**
```
[ESTILO] [ORYX] jumps and roars happily, wiggling his dinosaur tail. He opens a small
colorful notebook and sticks a shiny puppy sticker with a water drop inside, sparkles
appear. [LUNA] barks happily.
```

**Escena 10 · ¿Qué aprendimos? (10 s)**
```
[ESTILO] [ORYX] and [LUNA] sit in the shade under the tree with the water bowl next to
them, looking at the camera and talking happily.
```

**Escena 11 · Salida:** usar el clip de `../comun/cancion-entrada-salida.md`.

## Prompts de voz
- **Oryx:** `Young child voice, curious, cheerful, slightly high-pitched, amazed tone, Spanish.`
- **Luna:** `Sweet small female voice, gentle and brave, Spanish.`
