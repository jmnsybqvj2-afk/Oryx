# 🛸 Oryx and Luna — Channel bible

> The detailed, production-ready version of each episode is in the `episode-01-…`,
> `episode-02-…` and `episode-03-…` folders. See `README.md`.

Kids' channel (ages 0 to 6). Oryx, a little alien, comes to Earth to learn to care for, love and
respect animals and nature, together with his friend Luna, a chihuahua.

## Characters
Full description and prompt blocks: `shared/characters-and-style.md`.
Official image: `shared/references/oryx-and-luna-official.jpg`.

### 🟡 Oryx, the little alien
- Yellow skin, blue eyes, two gray antennae with yellow balls, green dinosaur pajamas with a
  dinosaur face on the hood and a tail. Spelled Oryx, pronounced "Oris".
- He comes from the Yellow Planet, where there are no animals or plants: everything on Earth amazes him.
- **Signature device:** when an animal needs help, his antennae light up red and go
  *"¡bip-bip-bip!"*. That's how every episode starts.
- **Catchphrase:** when he gets excited he roars like a dinosaur, *"¡RAWR!"*, and wiggles his tail.
- He often gets things wrong because he doesn't know the Earth yet, and the kids correct him.

### 🐕 Luna, the chihuahua
- Tiny, brown/tan, brave and very loving. Red collar with a golden bell ("tilín-tilín").
- **Signature device:** she understands animals and "translates" for Oryx how they feel.
- Sound: a short, high bark, *"¡Guau!"*, which means "I know!".

### 👩‍⚕️ Rosa, the veterinarian
The animals' doctor. She appears when the lesson is asking a grown-up for help.

### 📔 The Earthling Diary
At the end of every episode, Oryx sticks in a sticker of the animal he learned to care for.

## Structure of every episode (always the same, 3 to 5 min)

| Time | Moment | Length |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Opening song (always the same) | 15 s |
| 0:15 | Beep-beep! The animal with the problem appears | 15–20 s |
| 0:35 | Oryx gets it wrong (something funny) | 20–30 s |
| 1:00 | "¿Tú sabes qué necesita?" (Do you know what it needs?) + 3 s pause | 10 s |
| 1:10 | Luna translates and they solve it together | 30–60 s |
| 2:00 | Episode song | 1:00–1:30 |
| 3:15 | Celebration + sticker in the Earthling Diary | 15 s |
| 3:30 | "¿Qué aprendimos hoy?" (What did we learn today?) | 10 s |
| 3:40 | Closing song (always the same) | 20 s |
| **≈ 4:00** | **Total (range: 3:00 to 5:00)** | |

For a short episode (3 min), the episode song ends with a single chorus.
For a long one (5 min), Oryx gets it wrong twice before the solution.

## Ideas for the season
1. The slow turtle: Oryx learns patience
2. Trash in the river: picking it up and sorting it
3. The kitten that doesn't want to be picked up: respecting its space
4. Little bees: don't be afraid of them, they help the flowers!
5. Farm animal sounds
6. Luna's bath
7. The new little tree: planting a seed
8. The colors of animals
9. Counting ducklings from 1 to 5
10. The lost puppy: putting a name tag on your pet

## Strategy and rules
- Compilations of 30–60 min with 8–10 episodes.
- Each episode song as a standalone 1–2 min music video.
- Vertical 15–30 s Shorts with the "¡RAWR!" and the magical transformations.
- Publish at least once a week, always on the same day.
- Mark the channel as "Made for Kids" (no comments, no personalized ads).
- Educational, quality content; avoid repetitive or mass-produced content.
- Don't ask kids to subscribe or like; that goes to parents in the description.
- No character carries weapons.
