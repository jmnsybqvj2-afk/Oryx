# What works in successful kids' channels (and how Oryx and Luna uses it)

Research notes, November 2026. Ideas and patterns only: we never copy songs, characters, names or
episodes from other channels.

## Who leads
- **El Reino Infantil** (La Granja de Zenón, La Vaca Lola, Bartolito) is the most-watched
  Spanish-language kids' content on YouTube in 2026.
- **Pocoyo**, **CoComelon** and **Ms Rachel** are other references for preschool content.

## Patterns that work → how we use them

| What successful channels do | How Oryx and Luna does it |
|---|---|
| **Songs first** (El Reino Infantil started from the songs, then created characters and stories) | Every episode is built around its song, and each song is also published as its own music video and Short |
| **Animal characters with one signature sound or gag** (a rooster that imitates other animals, a cow everyone remembers) | Oryx's "¡bip-bip!" antennae and "¡RAWR!", Luna's bell "tilín-tilín". Future episodes can add recurring animal friends, each with one funny sound |
| **Simple values**: love for animals, caring for the land | Our core value: caring for, loving and respecting animals and nature |
| **Educators involved in the songs** | Each episode teaches one school skill (see `../channel-bible.md`). Ideally a preschool teacher reviews the songs |
| **Pause and wait for the child to answer** (Ms Rachel) | "¿Tú sabes qué necesita?" + a 3-second pause in every episode. Add more small pauses inside the songs ("¿De qué color es?… ¡ROJO!") |
| **Short, slow, simple language** ("parentese") | Dialogue lines stay short; Oryx and Luna speak slowly and clearly |
| **Labeling**: name the thing while showing it and pointing at it | Big words on screen (PÁJARO, NIDO…) while a character points at the object and says the word |
| **Repetition and a predictable format** | Same opening song, same structure, same closing song; the chorus repeats at least 3 times |
| **Recurring characters and theme songs feel safe** | Oryx, Luna, the saucer and the Earthling Diary appear in every episode |
| **Long compilations** that parents leave playing | Every 8–10 episodes: a 30–60 min compilation |

## New episode ideas from these patterns
- **Daily routines** (very popular in preschool channels): Oryx learns to brush his teeth, take a
  bath and go to bed — always with Luna and an animal lesson.
- **Animal sounds song**: Oryx doesn't know the sounds of Earth animals and Luna teaches him
  (cow, duck, sheep, horse…), with a sound-guessing pause for the kids.
- **Recurring animal friends**: a little duck, a turtle and a bee who come back in several
  episodes, each with one signature sound.

## What to avoid
- Ads and anything violent or scary (researchers point out these are more common on YouTube than
  on children's TV).
- Copying another channel's characters, songs or titles.
