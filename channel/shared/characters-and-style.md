# Characters and visual style

**The official image is `references/oryx-and-luna-official.jpg`.** This is how Oryx and Luna
always look. Use it as the reference in Kling (Elements).
- `references/oryx-and-luna-day.png` → the same characters **in daytime**. Use it as the
  reference or first frame for daytime scenes (park, garden).
- `references/channel-avatar-800.jpg` → channel profile picture (cropped from the official image).

## 🎨 Style
Hand-painted children's storybook illustration: gouache and colored-pencil texture, rich colors
(deep blues, vivid greens, warm yellows), soft warm light, round characters with big expressive
eyes. It feels cozy and gentle.
- The opening and closing happen **at night** with the moon and stars, like in the images. That
  way the dinosaur pajamas make sense and the closing works as a "good night".
- The stories happen **in daytime** (park, garden), in the same painted style.
- No fear and no real danger: problems are always solved with kindness.

## 🟡 Oryx, the little alien (based on the official image)
- **Name:** spelled **Oryx** (with an X) and pronounced **"Oris"**.
  When generating AI voices, write "Oris" in the dialogue text so it is pronounced correctly
  (if you write "Oryx", the voice may say "Óriks"). In titles, descriptions and on screen, always "Oryx".
- Yellow skin, rosy cheeks, big **blue** eyes with sparkles, open smile.
- **Two antennae**: thin gray stalks with **yellow balls**, poking out of the top of the hood.
  When an animal needs help, the balls **glow red** and go "¡bip-bip-bip!" (beep-beep-beep).
- **Green one-piece dinosaur pajamas**: the hood has a **dinosaur face with its own blue eyes**
  and small white teeth around Oryx's face, green spikes on the hood and back, light green
  striped belly, long tail with an arrow tip.
- Yellow hands and feet.
- Optional accessory: his brown **teddy bear** (it appears in the official image).
- When he gets excited he roars "¡RAWR!" and wiggles his tail.
- **Voice:** a child's voice, curious and cheerful, a little high-pitched.

## 🐕 Luna, the chihuahua
- Small **brown/tan** chihuahua puppy, cream chest and muzzle, big pointed ears (pink inside),
  big dark brown eyes, little tongue out when she is happy.
- **Red collar with a small golden bell.** The bell goes "tilín-tilín" when she runs: a sound
  kids learn to recognize.
- She understands animals and "translates" for Oryx what they feel.
- **Voice:** sweet and brave. Her short "¡Guau!" (woof) means "I know!".

## 🛸 The flying saucer
Classic flying saucer in lavender gray, with round yellow lights around the rim, a light blue
glass dome with a small antenna on top, and a warm yellow light beam underneath.
It has no image yet: generate it in Kling (see `kling-guide.md`).

## ☀️ Sol, the narrator
The friendly sun in the sky is the **narrator** of every episode, with the creator's cloned voice.
- **Welcome** (start of the episode): Sol, in a bright blue sky, opens big friendly eyes and talks.
- **Reflection** (end of the episode): the same Sol at **sunset** (orange sky) says goodbye and goes
  to sleep — just like Oryx at the end. We use Sol (not the moon) so kids don't confuse the moon
  with Luna the chihuahua.
- Look: the same round golden sun with soft rays from the episode images, with big kind eyes,
  rosy cheeks and a warm smile. Start frame: `../episode-01-pio-pio-pajarito/start-frames/start-sol-narrator.png`.

## 👩‍⚕️ Rosa, the veterinarian (episode 1)
Kind young woman, long brown hair, white veterinarian coat over a light blue shirt, blue gloves
and a stethoscope. She is the animals' doctor. She appears when the lesson is that **you ask a
grown-up for help**.
