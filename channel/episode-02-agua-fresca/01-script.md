# Episode 2 — "Luna Is Thirsty"

**Total length:** ≈ 4:00 · **Lesson:** our pets need fresh water and shade when it's hot.
**Characters:** Oryx and Luna.
**Place:** a garden with a big tree, on a very sunny day.

> Dialogue stays in **Spanish** (it is what the kids hear in the video). The English
> translation is in *italics* below each line.

---

### Scene 1 · 0:00–0:15 · Opening song
See `../shared/opening-and-closing-songs.md`.

### Scene 2 · 0:15–0:35 · Playing ball
[ON SCREEN: garden with a big sun. Oryx throws a red ball; Luna runs and brings it back.]
**Oryx:** ¡Otra vez, Luna! ¡Otra vez!
*(Again, Luna! Again!)*
[ON SCREEN: Luna brings the ball back more slowly, sits down and sticks out her little tongue, panting.]

### Scene 3 · 0:35–0:50 · Beep-beep!
[ON SCREEN: Oryx's antennae light up red.]
**Oryx:** ¡Bip-bip-bip! ¿Luna? ¡Mis antenitas suenan por ti!
*(Beep-beep-beep! Luna? My antennae are beeping for you!)*
**Oryx:** ¡Tienes la lengua afuera! ¿Es un juego nuevo?
*(Your tongue is sticking out! Is it a new game?)*

### Scene 4 · 0:50–1:10 · Oryx gets it wrong
**Oryx:** ¡Ya sé! ¡Te doy mi galleta espacial!
*(I know! I'll give you my space cookie!)*
[ON SCREEN: Oryx offers her a shiny star-shaped cookie. Luna turns her face away.]
**Luna:** ¡Guau! *(shakes her head)*
**Oryx:** ¿No quieres galleta? Mmm…
*(You don't want a cookie? Hmm…)*

### Scene 5 · 1:10–1:20 · Question for the kids
[ON SCREEN: Oryx looks at the camera. Three big options appear: 🍪 cookie, 💧 water, 🧸 toy.]
**Oryx:** ¿Tú sabes qué necesita Luna cuando hace mucho calor?
*(Do you know what Luna needs when it's very hot?)*
[ON SCREEN: 3-second pause. Then the 💧 glows and grows.]

### Scene 6 · 1:20–1:40 · Water!
**Oryx:** ¡¿Agua?! ¡Agua fresquita!
*(Water?! Nice fresh water!)*
**Luna:** ¡Guau! ¡Sí, Oryx! Cuando hace calor, los perritos necesitamos agua.
*(Woof! Yes, Oryx! When it's hot, us doggies need water.)*
[ON SCREEN: Oryx fills the bowl with a jug. Luna drinks happily: "glu, glu, glu".]

### Scene 7 · 1:40–2:00 · Into the shade
**Luna:** ¡Y también sombrita, para descansar del sol!
*(And some shade too, to rest from the sun!)*
[ON SCREEN: they both lie down under the tree. Luna wags her tail; Oryx fans himself with his hand.]
**Oryx:** ¡Aaah, qué fresquito!
*(Aaah, it's so nice and cool!)*

### Scene 8 · 2:00–3:15 · Song "¡Agua fresca!"
See `02-song.md`. Images for each part:
- **Verse 1:** the sun "quema, quema" (burns, burns), Luna with her tongue out.
- **Chorus:** Luna drinks and splashes little drops; Oryx jumps and dances.
- **Verse 2:** the space cookie bounces back, Oryx laughs.
- **Verse 3 and final chorus:** both under the tree, dancing in the shade.

### Scene 9 · 3:15–3:30 · Celebration and sticker
**Oryx:** ¡RAWR! ¡Estampita de perrito para mi Diario Terrícola!
*(RAWR! A puppy sticker for my Earthling Diary!)*
[ON SCREEN: he sticks in a puppy sticker with a water drop.]

### Scene 10 · 3:30–3:40 · What did we learn today?
**Oryx:** ¿Qué aprendimos hoy?
*(What did we learn today?)*
**Luna:** ¡Que cuando hace calor, las mascotas necesitamos agua fresca y sombrita!
*(That when it's hot, us pets need fresh water and shade!)*

### Scene 11 · 3:40–4:00 · Closing song
See `../shared/opening-and-closing-songs.md`.
