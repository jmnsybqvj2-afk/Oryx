### English translation (for you only, do not paste)
🐦 Oryx found a little bird that fell out of its nest! What does the little bird need? With the
help of Luna, his chihuahua, and Rosa the veterinarian, Oryx learns that little animals like to be
with their family.
🎵 Sing and dance "Tweet, tweet, little bird", and flap your wings!
👨‍👩‍👧 For parents: in this episode kids learn that if they find an animal that needs help, they
tell a grown-up first and never touch it on their own.
🛸 Oryx is a little alien who came to Earth to learn to care for, love and respect animals and
nature, together with his friend Luna.
