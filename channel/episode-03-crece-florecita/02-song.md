# Song — "Crece, crece, florecita"

**Style:** magical. It starts slow and soft, and each chorus gets a little faster and higher,
like the flower growing. **Length:** 1:15–1:30. **Tempo:** from ≈ 90 to ≈ 110 BPM.

> The lyrics are in **Spanish** (this is what is sung).

**Verse 1**
En el jardín hay una flor
triste, gris y sin color.
Oryx le canta rock espacial…
¡pero sigue igual, igual!

**Verse 2**
Luna huele la tierrita,
¡está seca, sequecita!
Le ponemos agüita,
¡plic, plic, plic!, poquito a poquito.

**Chorus**
Crece, crece, florecita,
con agüita y con sol,
crece, crece, despacito,
¡y se llena de color!

**Colors verse**
Se pone roja, se pone amarilla,
se pone rosa, ¡qué maravilla!
Se pone morada, se pone azul,
¡la flor más bonita eres tú!

**Chorus** (faster and louder) + ¡RAWR!

## Moves for the kids
- Start crouched down like a little seed.
- Make rain with your fingers on "plic, plic, plic".
- Stand up little by little during the chorus and end standing with your arms open like a flower.
- Point at something of each color in the colors verse.

## Prompt to generate the music
```
Magical children's song in Spanish, starts slow and soft at 90 BPM and gradually builds up
in energy and tempo to 110 BPM, key change up for the final chorus, celesta, harp,
glockenspiel, ukulele, sparkle and water drip "plic plic" sound effects, sweet child-like
vocals, joyful color-learning section, ends with a playful dinosaur roar.
```
If the app can't speed up the tempo, generate two versions (slow and fast) and join them in the
editing before the final chorus.

## Ways to use the song
1. Inside the episode (Scene 8).
2. A separate music video with the lyrics on screen. It also works as a video for learning colors.
3. A vertical Short, 20–30 s, with the gray-to-colors transformation.
